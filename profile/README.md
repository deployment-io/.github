Your coding agents got fast. Turning that code into shipped software didn't keep up — planning the work, coordinating changes across repos, standing up infrastructure, verifying everything works, and rolling out safely is still manual.

**Deployment.io runs that whole system.** You describe an outcome; our AI agents plan the work, build it across your repos, verify each change in a preview environment, and ship it to your cloud. You approve what reaches production.

## How it works

1. **Describe an outcome** — a feature, a migration, a fix.
2. **Plan** — our planning agent reads your repos and architecture, writes a spec, and breaks the outcome into ordered work across the right repos.
3. **Build** — agents write the code in sandboxed environments with scoped, least-privilege access to your cloud.
4. **Verify** — every change spins up a preview environment (frontend, API, and database) and is validated before it ships.
5. **Ship** — changes deploy to your cloud as static sites, web services, or full environments. Nothing reaches production without your approval.

## Bring your own coding agent

Prefer to drive the work yourself? Connect your own coding agent — **Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Copilot, OpenAI Codex, Gemini CLI**, and other SKILL.md-compliant agents — to Deployment.io over MCP. Native MCP tools give it the actions to deploy, preview, and manage environments; our open-source skill teaches it the playbook.

## Open source

The parts that touch your infrastructure are open — audit them, fork them, run your own:

- **[skills](https://github.com/deployment-io/skills)** — the SKILL.md-compliant skill that teaches any coding agent how to deploy on Deployment.io (MIT).
- **Open-source runner** — the runner that executes builds and deploys inside your own cloud. See the pinned repositories below.

## Two ways to use it

- **Self-serve** — run the platform yourself; metered by usage.
- **Managed** — our team operates Deployment.io for you, turning a backlog of outcomes into deployments on your cloud. [Learn more →](https://deployment.io/managed/)

## Your cloud, your control

Everything runs in your own cloud account through a scoped role you control — revoke it and we lose access instantly. Sandboxed execution, least-privilege access, approval gates by default, and a full audit trail.
